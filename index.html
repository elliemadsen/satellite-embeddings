Embedding Earth explores Google DeepMind's AlphaEarth Foundations satellite embedding dataset — a 64-dimensional vector representation of every 10m patch of land on Earth, derived from satellite imagery and other environmental input data.

Globe renders false-color embedding bands as a raster tile layer on a 3D globe. Topology applies dimension reduction algorithms to visualize globally-sampled embedding space as an interactive 3D scatter plot. Grid arranges dimensionally-reduced embedding space into tiled layouts with false color parametrization.

Land Classification data is sourced from Copernicus Global Dynamic Land Cover. Geographical subregions are from the United Nations geoscheme.
